One line of office comedy for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open". The joke takes the most familiar office complaint of all — the meeting that could have been an email — and turns it on itself: someone schedules a meeting to ask why the meeting couldn't have been an email, and the meeting itself replies-all to agree, cheerfully booking the next one for Thursday. The setup is a gripe everyone recognises, and the payoff lands in the last few words, when the meeting becomes a character with its own calendar.
